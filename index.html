Who we are

About the Lab

ARI² Lab (Autonomy, Robotics & Intelligent Interaction) brings together robotics, automation, and Embodied AI to develop intelligent systems capable of operating safely and reliably in the real world. Our research spans autonomous systems, multi-robot control, multimodal sensing, human–robot interaction, robot safety, robot learning, and spatial computing.

At the heart of our research is safe and resilient autonomy, from passively safe mechanical designs to intelligent control, AI guardrails, sensor redundancy, and robust control strategies for operation in complex, unstructured environments. Students gain hands-on experience designing, programming, integrating, and experimentally evaluating these technologies on real robotic systems.

Our research addresses challenges in energy, infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, healthcare, nursing, and other human-centered or safety-critical environments, creating opportunities for interdisciplinary research, industry partnerships, and regional collaboration.

What we work on

Research Areas

Our research spans five interconnected areas, from autonomous navigation and multi-robot coordination to intelligent interaction, resilient connectivity, and robotic safety.

01 / FIELD ROBOTICS

Autonomous Field Robotics

Advancing autonomous navigation, perception, and localization for robotic systems operating in complex and unstructured environments. Research encompasses multimodal sensor fusion, motion planning, and robust autonomy under uncertain environmental conditions.

Representative projects
  • LiDAR-inertial SLAM
  • GNSS-denied navigation
  • Vision-language navigation
  • Terrain-aware motion planning
02 / MULTI-ROBOT SYSTEMS

Heterogeneous Multi-Robot Systems

Developing coordinated control and distributed autonomy for heterogeneous robotic teams comprising legged robots, mobile manipulators, and aerial robots. Research addresses cooperative sensing, relative localization, and control strategies for collaborative tasks involving robots with complementary capabilities.

Representative projects
  • Multi-robot cluster-space control
  • Decentralized coordination and control policies
  • Cooperative manipulation
03 / HUMAN–ROBOT INTERACTION

Spatial Computing and Shared Autonomy

Integrating spatial computing and intelligent control for local and remote human–robot interaction. Research encompasses augmented and mixed reality, robot intent visualization, intelligent constraints, and human-in-the-loop shared autonomy.

Representative projects
  • AR-based robot interaction
  • Constrained teleoperation
  • Preference-aware shared autonomy
04 / RESILIENT CONNECTIVITY

Adaptive and Resilient Connectivity

Investigating adaptive wireless communication and autonomous deployment of mobile communication infrastructure in remote and infrastructure-limited environments. Research addresses decentralized network coordination, communication-aware robot positioning, and dynamic network reconfiguration.

Representative projects
  • Elastic Mobile Infrastructure on Demand (eMID)
  • UWB-based localization
  • Self-reconfiguring robotic mesh networks
05 / ROBOT SAFETY

Safe and Compliant Robotic Systems

Investigating compliant actuation and variable-stiffness mechanisms for safe physical human–robot interaction. Research addresses passive mechanical safety, interaction-dependent stiffness regulation, and safety-performance tradeoffs in collaborative and wearable robotic systems.

Representative projects
  • Variable Series Elastic Actuators (vSEA)
  • Passively safe robotic joints
  • Interaction-dependent stiffness control
